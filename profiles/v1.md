## What they build
Hera Health Solutions builds bioerodible drug delivery devices using a proprietary nanotechnology-based platform. These devices are composed of non-woven sheets made from bioerodible polymers and active agents, which are obtained through electrospinning a solution with a concentration of 1-100mg/ml. The devices are designed to deliver medicines steadily over days, months, or years and can be formulated as implants, patches, or other delivery formats. The platform is capable of delivering both small molecules and large biologics, and can be fine-tuned to match different therapeutic needs.

## What they do NOT do
Hera Health Solutions does not discover new drugs or develop new active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs). They reformulate existing therapeutics into long-acting implants, patches, and other delivery formats using their proprietary nanotechnology-enabled drug delivery platform. They do not develop oral dosing or current long-acting injectables, instead focusing on customizable, finely tuned drug release profiles using biodegradable nanofiber scaffolds.
